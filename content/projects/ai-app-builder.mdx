The system behind the [Building an AI App Builder](../../series/ai-app-builder/) series.

A chat that writes Next.js apps into a live workspace pod. A CrewAI crew (builder,
researcher, reviewer, a designer that critiques real screenshots) does the work;
cost guards cap every generation at a fixed number of repair attempts and a wall
clock; prompts are versioned in Langfuse with the code as sanctioned fallback.

Status: working end to end on local infrastructure, being hardened one broken
generation at a time. The series is the build log.
